= ephemeral | adj | lasting a very short time | enduring
ephemeral | 1 | lasting for a very short time | Fame on social media is often ephemeral, gone within a week.
transient | 1 | lasting only for a short time; impermanent | The drug caused only transient side effects that disappeared within hours.
transitory | 1 | not permanent | Their happiness proved transitory, ending with the harvest failure.
evanescent | 2 | soon passing out of sight or memory; vanishing | The evanescent glow of the sunset faded before we could photograph it.
fleeting | 2 | lasting for a very short time | She caught a fleeting glimpse of the fox before it vanished.
fugitive | 3 | quick to disappear; fleeting | The poem tries to capture fugitive moments of joy.
> contrast | Although the fashion seemed at first to be a lasting change in taste, it proved ____, vanishing within a single season. | "Although … lasting … vanishing within a single season" — short-lived.
> continuation | The mayfly's life is famously ____: most adults live for only a single day. | "Live for only a single day" defines the blank.

> continuation | The flood's effect on the survivors has been ____: decades later, they still speak of it as though it happened yesterday. | "Decades later … still speak of it" — long-lasting.
